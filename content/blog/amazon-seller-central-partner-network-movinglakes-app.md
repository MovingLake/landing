We are thrilled to announce today that MovingLake’s App is now available through Amazon Seller Central Partner Network.

[You can see the listing here](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.mx/selling-partner-appstore/dp/amzn1.sp.solution.2f085082-86de-47f6-b772-6df8d3fc4924)

The Amazon Seller Central Partner Network is a one-stop-shop where the small and medium-sized businesses that sell in the Amazon store can easily discover quality applications to help them automate, manage and grow their business. Accessible from Seller Central, the Amazon Seller Central Partner Network features applications and service solutions created by Amazon, as well as solutions from external software or service partners, and covers a range of functionalities across the selling lifecycle.

With MovingLake’s App any business which sells on Amazon can now extract data from their account about:

1. Orders
2. Inventory
3. Fulfillment / Shipping
4. Financial fees and reporting
5. Catalog items
6. Shipping labels

And other information available from the Amazon Seller Partner API. Together with MovingLake’s Amazon Advertising API connector, businesses which sell on Amazon can now get a real-time view of their seller account and performance of their products.
