## Analytical vs. transactional

There is no one-size-fits-all solution when it comes to databases, data lakes and data warehouses. Each has different benefits and drawbacks.

**Analytical data stores (OLAP)** include the data warehouse vendors such as Snowflake, Redshift and BigQuery. They are normally columnar databases optimized for storing vast amounts of data at a decent price while letting you issue complex queries to understand your business data. Using one of these for operational workloads with lots of updates would be costly and inefficient.

**Transactional data stores** are used for operational workloads such as managing all the data from a web application. Examples are MongoDB, PostgreSQL and MySQL. These databases can handle a huge volume of updates as users interact with the product.
